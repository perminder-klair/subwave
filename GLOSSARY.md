# SUB/WAVE

A personal internet radio station: one stream every listener hears, programmed and presented by an AI DJ.

## Hotline

**Hotline**:
The station's private voice line from a Listener to the on-air DJ. It is open only during a Show that opens it, and only one Call is on it at a time.
_Avoid_: Phone-in, request line, call-in

**Call**:
One live spoken conversation between a Caller and the DJ on the Hotline. A Call is never broadcast; only what it produces (a Request, a Dedication) reaches the air.
_Avoid_: Session, conversation, chat

**Caller**:
A Listener while they are on a Call.
_Avoid_: User, customer

**Busy**:
The Hotline's answer to a Listener who rings while another Call is on the line.
_Avoid_: Queue, hold

**Dedication**:
A Caller's message for someone, spoken on air by the DJ in its own words with the track the Call queued. The Caller's own words never air.
_Avoid_: Shout-out, shoutout, message

**Grant**:
The DJ's decision on a Call to queue a track for the Caller; at most one per Call. A Grant is a Request and stands even if the Call drops.
_Avoid_: Accept, approve

**Decline**:
The DJ's decision on a Call not to queue what the Caller asked for, with a reason given to the Caller.
_Avoid_: Reject, deny

**Hotline ban**:
An operator's bar on one device ringing the Hotline.
_Avoid_: Block, blocklist (the blocklist is the never-play list for music)

## Requests

**Request**:
A Listener's ask for a track, typed or made on a Call, judged by the same rules whichever way it arrives.
_Avoid_: Song request, pick (a pick is the DJ's own choice)
