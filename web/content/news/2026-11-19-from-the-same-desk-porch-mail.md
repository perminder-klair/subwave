This dispatch is about something that is not SUB/WAVE, so here is the disclosure first. I make SUB/WAVE, and I also make Porch Mail. Porch Mail is a paid app. Its sales help pay for the time that goes into this project, which is why it is now listed as a sponsor.

## What Porch Mail is

[Porch Mail](https://www.porch.email/?ref=subwave) is a desktop email app for Mac, Windows and Linux. It works with the mailbox you already have: Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail, Proton through its Bridge, or anything that speaks IMAP or JMAP.

The idea it is built on is the Gate. The first email from a sender you have never heard from does not land in your inbox. It waits at the Gate until you decide where that sender belongs: with the people, with the newsletters, with the receipts, or blocked. You decide once per sender, and their later mail goes straight there.

Version 1.0 came out on 15 November.

## Why it might interest you

If you run your own radio station from your own music library, you probably care about the same things Porch Mail was built around.

- **Your mail stays between you and your provider.** The app talks to your mailbox directly. There is no Porch server in between and no account to create with us.
- **No telemetry.** The app has no analytics and no crash reporter.
- **AI is off until you pick a model.** It can be a local one, through Ollama or LM Studio, the same way SUB/WAVE's DJ can run on your own hardware.
- **Agents can draft, and only you can send.** There is a command-line tool, a terminal interface and an MCP server, for the same crowd that drives [the booth from an agent](/news/agent-in-the-booth).

It is free to use in full for 7 days. After that it is one payment, with no subscription: $35 at the launch price as this goes to press.

One thing is different from SUB/WAVE, and it is better said plainly. Porch Mail is not open source.

## What changes for SUB/WAVE

Nothing in the software.

SUB/WAVE stays open source and free to run. The advertisement is one labelled box near the foot of the landing page, and it appears only on getsubwave.com. Every install serves the same landing page, so the box checks which site it is on and stays off yours. It is a plain link, with no tracking script behind it.
