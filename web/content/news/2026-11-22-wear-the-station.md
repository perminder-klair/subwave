There is a shop now. It lives at [shop.getsubwave.com](https://shop.getsubwave.com), and you can browse it, fill a bag and talk to the DJ behind the counter. You can't buy anything yet. The checkout goes to a page called Dead air, which says the till isn't wired up. Nothing is charged, and your bag is still there when you go back.

## What's on the shelves

The shelves come in three sections.

**The station.** A black organic cotton tee with "No skip button" on the back, a heavyweight charcoal 77.00 FM hoodie, an ON AIR mug and a pack of six vinyl stickers for the laptop, the NAS, the rack and the fridge.

**For the homelab.** A laser-etched 1U rack blanking panel, a desk mat made to order from your own [Library Observatory](/manual/observatory) map, a lossless tote, a 77.00 FM cap and an ON AIR enamel pin. One is a resin keycap called The skip key, which the listing tells you to map to nothing. The page admits that some of these are jokes, and that most of the jokes are also real products.

**The hardware drop.** A vinyl skin kit for the GPU that runs your local DJ, and a pair of clip-on RAM heatspreader covers. One reads "Timings: no skip", the other "The DJ lives here".

Every item says it would be printed to order, so nothing sits in a warehouse.

## The DJ at the counter

The shop has a counter on channel 7700, the port SUB/WAVE's web player uses. Ask the DJ what to get a homelab nerd, or whether the hoodie is warm, or just say what you want and it goes in the bag. It is the same idea as the station: an AI host who knows what's on the shelves, in place of a search box.

## Why it isn't open

We'd rather not print a run of hoodies nobody wants. The shop is up first to find out which of these things people would actually buy. If one of them is for you, say so on [Discord](https://discord.gg/vjVbVKnMBa) or [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/getsubwave/), and name the item. The ones people ask for are the ones that get made.

You'll find the shop in the Supplements on the [landing page](/) and in the links at the foot of every page.
