New-user-style Show Brief
Bob's Alternative Afternoon is packed with great guitar music from the 80s, 90s and 2000s, mixing alternative rock, indie, Britpop and classic rock. Play plenty of Bob's favourite artists such as R.E.M., The Cure, The Smiths, Radiohead, Oasis, Blur and The Stone Roses, alongside similar artists and newer discoveries. Bob loves melodic guitars, acoustic tracks, strong basslines and interesting production, so favour songs with those qualities. Avoid manufactured pop, repetitive dance music and novelty records. Don't just play the obvious hits — dig deeper into albums and find forgotten favourites, overlooked gems and tracks listeners might not have heard for years. Keep the music varied but recognisably Bob, with a thoughtful, knowledgeable feel and enough energy for the afternoon.
