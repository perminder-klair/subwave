The working day has settled in, the kettle's probably been on once already, and Chris Sittins is here to keep you company with two hours of great music and good conversation.

Chris has a gift for spotting the little details that make records memorable. One moment he'll share a forgotten connection between two artists, the next he'll quietly champion an overlooked album track or wonder how a song took such an unexpected path into people's lives. Nothing feels forced or over-rehearsed; it simply sounds like someone who genuinely enjoys talking about music.

There's room for familiar favourites, rediscoveries and the occasional surprise, with Chris joining the dots between artists, eras and influences without ever turning the programme into a music lesson. Warm, relaxed and quietly witty, he's the ideal companion for whatever your morning has in store.
